Publications CV (PDF)

Research

Urban digital twins and complexity

How far can a model of a city be trusted, and for whom is it built? Work on validating a large-scale digital twin of traffic with empirical data, on the limits and ethics of twins and metaverses, and on how pruning street networks changes city-making.

Barcelona traffic twin, twins and metaverses, pruning street networks

Participatory and pluralistic AI

Language-model agents, digital twins and open data used to widen participation in planning and in democratic deliberation, from a replica of deliberative democracy to townhall-scale planning and routing that serves more than one objective.

digital twins for deliberative democracy, LLM agents for participatory planning, open government data with LLMs

Perception and the built environment

Experiments on how people see and talk about places: virtual-reality studies of future streets, semantic urban elements, socio-spatial inequities in urban greenery, hallucination as a measure of cognitive friction, and augmented-reality installations.

VR study of future streets, semantic urban elements, urban greenery inequities

Cooperative mobility

Multi-agent and spatial models of traffic and accessibility: priority and adaptable lanes for mixed traffic, bike-sharing accessibility across cities, and the traffic effects of turning office space into housing.

priority lane management, bike-sharing across 40 cities, office space into housing

Semantic Urban Elements

Paper

In the Eyes Of

An offline-first system for geolocalized expert narration in heritage interpretation, with Virginia Tech collaborators. Poster at Meta's Research Summit for Egocentric Intelligence, July 2026.

Code Software (Zenodo)

Treespotting AR (TSAR)

A monochromatic point-cloud installation in augmented reality, learning from Cesare Leonardi and Franca Stagi's graphics of trees. It reduces data to increase phenomenological engagement with urban nature, and was field-tested by 430 participants. Shown in Modena and Karlsruhe in 2025 and presented at the Digital Landscape Architecture conference in Dublin in 2026.

Paper

Co-Evolving City Life

An ERC Advanced Grant project at the Professorship of Computational Social Science on decentralised, participatory approaches to city life.

Project page CORDIS Featured in an EU report

A Switzerland of Only Ten Million?

An interactive commune-level analysis of the 14 June 2026 Swiss vote on capping the population at ten million: a map and the correlates of the Yes share across every commune, built with D3 on public data from the Federal Statistical Office and swisstopo.
